Visa

Types of visas and basic requirements:

  • Working Holiday Visa: Usually limited in duration and requires proof of sufficient funds, health coverage, and a valid passport.
  • Student Visa: Must provide a certificate of enrollment from a German university and proof of financial means.
  • Family Reunion Visa: Requires legal documents proving the relationship and sufficient financial support.
  • Tourist Visa (long stay): Only allowed for a limited period; proof of accommodation and funds is needed.

Health & Insurance

  • Germany requires basic emergency insurance for all visa applications.
  • For any medical treatment beyond emergencies, you need a German health insurance, usually obtained once you have employment.
  • If planning to live in Germany long-term, it is recommended to have liability insurance (“Haftpflichtversicherung”) for accidents or damages. This typically costs 60–90 € per year.

Language

Many people say that English is enough, but in reality, very few locals speak or prefer English. In large cities, you might manage with English, but there will still be gaps in medical appointments, bureaucracy, and official procedures. If planning to live in Germany, it is strongly recommended to learn German as soon as possible.
